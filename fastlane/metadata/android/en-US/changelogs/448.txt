PGPony 4.5.3 fixes a device-specific problem where keys could stop working after a few days; key storage is now on a more resilient foundation and existing keys migrate automatically the first time they are used after updating. This release also adds file signing with ML-DSA keys and verifies ML-DSA signatures on files and through mail apps, fixes a decrypted attachment being wrongly reported as incomplete, shows whether a signature's signer key is confirmed rather than a flat "verified", and stops expired keys from being used to sign or encrypt unless you enable that in Settings. Signature verification in the share Quick Action is fixed as well.
