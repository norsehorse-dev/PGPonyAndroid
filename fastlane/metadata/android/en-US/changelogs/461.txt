Fixes encrypting from the share menu to a post-quantum (ML-DSA) key or a v4 key with an ML-KEM subkey. Alone, such a recipient failed with "no recipient keys"; next to other recipients it was left out without warning, so the file could not be opened by that person. The share menu now loads every recipient the way the Encrypt screen does, and refuses to encrypt if any selected key cannot be used. Arm memory tagging (MTE) is on where the phone supports it.
