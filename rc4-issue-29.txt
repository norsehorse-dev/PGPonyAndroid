Earlier in this thread I said multiple identities would be 4.3.0. I was wrong in the good direction: it landed in 4.2.0 RC3. The Key Detail restructure this cycle put all the plumbing in place, so holding the feature back a full release stopped making sense.

Key Detail can now add User IDs, revoke them, and change which one is primary. Exports carry the full identity list, and gpg reads everything back correctly, including revocations. One thing worth knowing when you verify: gpg hides fully revoked User IDs from its default listing; pass --list-options show-unusable-uids to see them marked revoked.

On your K-9 finding: thank you for narrowing it to v4 works, v5/v6 does not, and for re-checking on 4.1.1. Your read matches mine. K-9 and Thunderbird for Android still parse keys on their side of the OpenPGP API, and their parser predates the newer key versions, so there is nothing PGPony can do from the provider side to make a v6 key acceptable to them. For mail through K-9, a v4 key is the interop path until they catch up. I test the provider against K-9, FairEmail, and Thunderbird before every release and will keep an eye on when their v6 support lands; this caveat is going in the release notes and FAQ so the next person does not have to discover it the way you did.
