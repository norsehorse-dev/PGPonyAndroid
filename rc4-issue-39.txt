Good call, and the pieces are already there, just not linked from the README. Every release is built reproducibly and F-Droid rebuilds each one to confirm it matches; the full procedure lives in REPRODUCIBLE_BUILDS_PLAYBOOK.md and docs/REPRODUCIBLE_BUILDS.md, and tools/verify_repro.sh is the gate script that clones a tag twice, proves the builds are byte-identical, and compares against a candidate APK.

I have added a 'Verify a release' section to the README that pulls this together: how to check a downloaded APK's signature against the release certificate fingerprint, what the two published hashes mean, and how to rebuild from a tag and compare. The signing certificate SHA-256 is 446bf9e621222a40c66cd2476e1a97105ccb2a9b16a01a91c1c7eb90765b50dc, verifiable with apksigner verify --print-certs on any release APK.
