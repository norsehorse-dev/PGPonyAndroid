RC3 adds the delete safeguards. Deleting a key pair now goes through a sheet that offers to save an .asc backup first, requires ticking "I understand this cannot be undone" before Delete enables, and, if you use the biometric app lock, asks for a fingerprint at the end. Public-only keys keep the one-tap dialog, since those can be re-imported.
