Thanks for the list, this is a good one. Most of it is going into 4.4.0.

Going in: the email fields become tap-to-copy in Key Detail, both the header address and the User IDs. The bottom Keyring button will pop back to the Keyring from Key Detail, which is where your thumb already expects it. Export private key goes back to the yellow warning color, since it is a sensitive action and the grey lost that signal. The groups get their counts in the header, "My Keys (6)" and so on. And the default key gets marked on the Keyring so you can pick it out at a glance.

The two larger ones are in for 4.4.0 as well: collapsible group lists, which also means you can open the app without your private keys on screen, and a search that matches any part of the name, key ID, or fingerprint.

None of it is complex, as you said, but together it makes the Keyring a lot nicer to live in. Thanks for thinking it through so clearly.

The Keyring pass is in RC3.

Emails are tap-to-copy in Key Detail, both the header address and each User ID. The bottom Keyring tab returns you to the list from Key Detail and reads as the active tab while you are there. Export private key is the yellow warning color again, on the row and in the overflow menu. Section headers carry their counts, "My Keys (6)". The default key wears a DEFAULT pill on its card. Groups collapse, and the collapsed state sticks, so you can open the app with My Keys folded away. And there is a search that matches any part of the name, key ID, or fingerprint.

https://pgpony.app/rc/4.4.0-RC-foss/PGPony-4.4.0-RC3-foss.apk

Installs over your current build, no uninstall. To retest: tap an email to copy it, collapse My Keys and reopen the app, and search by a piece of a fingerprint.
