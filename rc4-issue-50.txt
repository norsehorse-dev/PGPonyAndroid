Good call, and both directions are in RC3.

Biometric lock and require fingerprint to sign now ask for a fingerprint before they change, turning them on as well as off. Enabling prompts too, so a fingerprint that will not read is caught then rather than locking you out later. On a device with no enrolled biometric the toggles still work, so no one is stranded without a way to change them.

https://pgpony.app/rc/4.4.0-RC-foss/PGPony-4.4.0-RC3-foss.apk

Installs over your current build, no uninstall. To retest: turn each toggle off and back on, confirm the fingerprint prompt each time, and that cancelling leaves the switch where it was.
