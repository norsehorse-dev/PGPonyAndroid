It is in RC2, up now.

On a v6 key, Add Subkey now offers Ed25519 (Authenticate) next to Sign and Encrypt. On a v4 key you also get RSA 2048/4096 (Authenticate). The subkey carries the Authentication key flag, so it exports as a normal auth-capable subkey that gpg-agent and anything reading key flags will treat as an SSH authentication key.

https://pgpony.app/rc/4.4.0-RC-foss/PGPony-4.4.0-RC2-foss.apk

Installs over your current build, no uninstall.
