4.3.0 RC2 is up, and it adds the passphrase change you asked for in this
issue:

https://pgpony.app/rc/4.3.0-RC-foss/PGPony-4.3.0-RC2-foss.apk

The sha256 and the detached signature (.asc) sit beside it. It installs in
place over your current copy, F-Droid included, since the reproducible
build is signed with the same key.

On a software key pair, Key Detail now has a "Change passphrase" row next
to Export. It sets a passphrase on a key that has none, changes an existing
one, or removes it entirely (leave the new passphrase blank, behind a
confirmation). It works on every key type. If you have a composite ML-KEM
key, that is the most useful one to test, in both the v6 and LibrePGP
forms.

The retest that helps most: change the passphrase on one of your keys, then
use that key afterward, decrypt something to it and sign with it, to
confirm it still works under the new passphrase. The set and remove
directions are worth a try too. One thing to know: an existing backup keeps
its old passphrase until you export the key again.
