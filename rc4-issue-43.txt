Nitrokey 3 should already work. PGPony talks to standard OpenPGP cards over both NFC and USB-OTG, and the Nitrokey 3 is one of the cards it was built against, so it is a listed target rather than a stretch.

The honest part: I do not own one yet, so I have not confirmed it on real hardware. I ordered a Nitrokey 3, but it is on backorder. It was meant to ship in June, then slipped to September the last time I checked.

So if you already have one and are willing to test, that would help a lot. Try it over NFC, or plugged in over USB-OTG, and tell me what you see: whether the card is detected, and whether signing and decrypting go through. If anything fails I will fold the fix into the next release. As far as the code goes it should work today, I just have no way to confirm it myself yet.
