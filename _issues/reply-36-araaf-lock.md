@AraafRoyall the protection you are after is already in, in layers, so I am going to leave it there rather than add a full lock.

With Protect destructive actions on (the default), deleting a key, removing a subkey, or clearing data asks for your device unlock first, so a stray tap cannot destroy anything. Deleting a key also sends it to Recently Deleted for 14 days, and the delete sheet offers a backup before it goes. So an accidental delete is stopped in the moment, recoverable for two weeks after, and you are nudged to back up before it happens at all.

A switch that fully greys the options out would sit on top of all that, and it would mean opening Settings to flip it off every time you did want to delete something. That is more friction than safety at that point, so I am not going to add it. If you ever run into something getting deleted that those three layers did not catch, tell me exactly how it happened and I will look, since that would be a real gap rather than a missing guard.
